# PulseGym Member Rules

Version 3.2. Every rule has an ID. Your assistant must cite the IDs it used.
**The Updates section at the end overrides any older rule it mentions.**

---

## 1. Memberships

**R-01 Membership types and prices.**
PulseGym offers three memberships: Monthly (EGP 1,200 per month), Quarterly (EGP 3,300 for 3 months) and Annual (EGP 11,000 for 12 months). Students with a valid university ID get a 15% discount on Quarterly and Annual memberships. The student discount does not apply to Monthly memberships.

**R-02 Payments and renewal.**
Payment is due on the membership start date. Monthly memberships renew automatically every month unless the member cancels at least 7 days before the renewal date. Quarterly and Annual memberships do not renew automatically.

**R-03 Minimum age.**
Members must be at least 16 years old. Members aged 16 or 17 need a consent form signed by a parent or guardian.

**R-04 Personal access.**
Each membership card and app QR code is personal. Sharing it with anyone else leads to a 30-day suspension without a refund.

## 2. Freezing a membership

**R-05 Freeze limits.**
Annual members can freeze their membership for up to 60 days per membership year, in blocks of at least 14 days. Quarterly members can freeze once, for up to 14 days. Monthly memberships cannot be frozen.

**R-06 How to freeze.**
A freeze must be requested at least 3 days before it starts, through the app or at the front desk. A medical freeze, with a doctor's note, can be requested at any time, does not count toward the limits in R-05, and can last up to 90 days.

## 3. Guests

**R-07 Guest visits.**
Members can bring a guest up to 2 times per calendar month. Each guest visit costs EGP 150. Annual members also get 4 free guest passes per membership year.

**R-08 Guest requirements.**
Guests must be at least 18, show a valid ID and sign a waiver at the front desk. The same guest can visit at most 3 times per calendar month, whoever invites them.

## 4. Class booking

**R-09 Booking window.**
Classes can be booked through the app up to 7 days in advance.

**R-10 Booking limits.**
A member can hold at most 2 active class bookings per day and at most 8 per week (Saturday to Friday).

**R-11 Cancelling a class.**
A class booking can be cancelled without penalty up to 2 hours before the class starts. A later cancellation, or not showing up, counts as a strike. A member with 3 strikes within 30 days cannot book classes for the next 7 days.

**R-12 Arriving on time.**
Members should arrive 5 minutes before the class. If a member has not checked in by 5 minutes after the start time, their spot is released to the waitlist.

**R-13 Waitlist.**
When a class is full, members can join its waitlist. When a spot opens, the next member on the waitlist is notified in the app and has 30 minutes to confirm. If they don't confirm in time, the spot goes to the next member.

## 5. Cancelling a membership and refunds

**R-14 Monthly cancellation.**
Monthly memberships are not refundable. A member can cancel at any time; the membership stays active until the end of the paid month, subject to the 7-day notice in R-02.

**R-15 Quarterly and Annual cancellation.**
A Quarterly or Annual membership cancelled within 14 days of purchase gets a full refund if the member visited fewer than 3 times. After 14 days, Annual members are refunded the remaining full months minus a 10% admin fee. Quarterly memberships are not refundable after 14 days.

**R-16 Refund timing.**
Refunds are paid within 10 business days to the original payment method.

## 6. Opening hours and facilities

**R-17 Opening hours.**
Saturday to Thursday: 06:00 to 24:00. Friday: 13:00 to 23:00. Public holidays: 10:00 to 22:00.

**R-18 Women-only hours.**
The main gym floor is women-only every day from 10:00 to 13:00, except Friday.

**R-19 Towels and lockers.**
Towels are free. Day lockers are free; anything left in a day locker overnight is removed. A personal locker can be rented for EGP 200 per month.

**R-20 Personal training.**
Personal training sessions are not included in any membership and are booked and paid separately at the front desk.

## 7. Health and safety

**R-21 Injuries and medical questions.**
Members must report any injury to staff immediately. Staff and the assistant do not give medical advice. For pain, injuries or medical conditions, members should see a doctor; a PulseGym trainer can then adjust their training plan.
