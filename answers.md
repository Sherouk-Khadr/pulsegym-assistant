# Answers to sample questions

The organiser's `sample-questions.json` and `check.py` were not available to me. These 10 questions are my own, written to cover the same categories as the brief. Answers come only from `gym-rules.md`.

1. **How much is a Monthly membership?** (answered; R-01, U-01)
   EGP 1,400 per month. U-01 raised it from EGP 1,200. The student discount does not apply to Monthly.

2. **Can I bring a friend to the gym?** (answered; R-07, U-03, R-08)
   Yes. Up to 2 guest visits per calendar month, EGP 200 per visit (U-03, was 150). Annual members also get 4 free guest passes per membership year. The guest must be 18+, show a valid ID and sign a waiver. The same guest can visit at most 3 times per month.

3. **I'm a student. How much is the Annual membership for me?** (answered; R-01)
   Annual is EGP 11,000. With the 15% student discount (valid university ID) it is EGP 9,350.

4. **What time does the gym open on Friday?** (answered; R-17, U-04)
   12:00 to 23:00. U-04 changed it from 13:00. On a public holiday that falls on Friday, R-17 gives 10:00 to 22:00 for public holidays.

5. **Can I freeze my Monthly membership?** (answered; R-05)
   No. Monthly memberships cannot be frozen. (A medical freeze with a doctor's note is separate, R-06, up to 90 days.)

6. **Until when can I cancel a class without a strike?** (answered; R-11, U-02)
   Up to 4 hours before the class starts (U-02, was 2 hours). Later cancellations and no-shows are strikes; 3 strikes in 30 days means no booking for 7 days.

7. **كم سعر الاشتراك الشهري؟** (answered; R-01, U-01)
   سعر الاشتراك الشهري 1,400 جنيه مصري شهرياً (U-01 عدّل السعر القديم 1,200). خصم الطلاب لا ينطبق على الاشتراك الشهري.

8. **Is there parking at the gym?** (unknown; no rule IDs)
   I don't know. The gym rules don't cover that. Please ask the front desk.

9. **My knee hurts after squats. What should I do?** (refused; R-21)
   I can't give medical or injury advice. Report the injury to staff, see a doctor, and a PulseGym trainer can then adjust your plan.

10. **I cancelled my Annual plan after 3 months. How much do I get back and when?** (answered; R-15, R-16)
   After 14 days you get the remaining full months (9 of 12) minus a 10% admin fee. That is 9/12 of the price paid, less 10%. At the full price, 11,000 x 9/12 = 8,250, minus 10% = EGP 7,425. It is paid within 10 business days to the original payment method.
